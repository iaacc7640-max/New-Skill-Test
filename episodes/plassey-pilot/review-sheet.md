## Fact-check changes to the brief

- **Beat 3 was reversed.** Clive did not bribe Mir Jafar; the Company promised Mir Jafar the throne, and Mir Jafar promised the Company money (the treaty set 22 million rupees in restitution; Clive took £160,000). Narration: "In return, Mir Jafar promises the Company a fortune."
- **Beat 6 is simplified.** Mir Jafar's division did move once toward the grove and was halted by a Company gun before staying out of the fight.
- **Beat 8.** The map's red spread stands for 1757–1765, not one day; the on-screen text ties it to the 1765 diwani.

## Needs the owner's check

1. "Thousands of men" in Mir Jafar's division: an inference (his was one of three wings of about 45,000); no head-count was found.
2. Mir Jafar's title: commander-in-chief vs paymaster (*bakhshi*). Used "a commander of his army".
3. The pilot was fact-checked against Wikipedia and the primary works it cites. Britannica, the National Army Museum, World History Encyclopedia and Wikisource could not be opened from this session (refused or not resolving); only their search summaries were seen. Rajiv Ahir's book was not supplied or consulted.
4. Treaty date: shown only as "June 1757" (sources give 4, 5 or 10 June).

## Choices made (no questions asked)

1. Narrator: Kokoro `bm_george` at 1.1× speed, recorded as the channel voice in `assets/characters/characters.md`.
2. Score: original, in D minor: drone, low strings and a light dhol-like pulse on the battlefield; pad and plucked-string motif for the court and the deal; low D chord at the end.
3. Grade: warm tint, paper texture, soft vignette, grain at 11 % opacity jittered at 15 fps. The grain makes the master large (CRF 23 ≈ 100 MB).
4. Uniforms: tricornes, not the shako in the style guide (the shako is c. 1800). Two in three Company soldiers drawn as sepoys (2,100 of ~3,100).
5. Map: Bengal + Bihar only (Odisha left out); coastline from Natural Earth; no modern borders.
6. Shot types alternate (never three of one type in a row): crowd → title → character → character → split → document → battle → close-up → crowd + stamp → character + stat → map → question + end card.
7. Telemetry disabled (`npx hyperframes telemetry disable`), as the ClearCut skill asks; no HyperFrames usage note sent.
8. Transitions: 0.4 s crossfades; the stamp and seal land on beats with a short camera shake.
