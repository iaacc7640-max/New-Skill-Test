# Packaging — The Deal at Plassey (style pilot)

**Title (under 45 characters, no dates or series tags):** The Battle Decided Before a Shot Was Fired

Alternative: How One Man Standing Still Won Bengal

## Description

Three thousand Company soldiers against roughly fifty thousand. On the plain at Plassey, the small army won, but the battle was decided weeks earlier in a secret deal. This 90-second pilot shows Siraj ud-Daulah, Mir Jafar, Robert Clive, a monsoon storm and a betrayal that handed Bengal to the East India Company.
